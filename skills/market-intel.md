# /market-intel — Competitive Intelligence & Customer Discovery

## Purpose
Understand the market before entering it. Map competitors, discover customer needs, test pricing, identify gaps, and design a positioning strategy. Prevents the #1 cause of business failure: building something nobody wants to buy at the price you need to charge.

## Trigger
User says: /market-intel [product/service and location]

## Input Required
ONE sentence. Examples:
- "Poultry farming in Kumasi"
- "Mobile phone repair in Tamale"
- "Shea butter export from Northern Ghana"
- "Catering for offices in Accra"

### STEP 5 — Your Competitive Advantage

```
POSITIONING STATEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Who [need/problem they have]
Our [product/service]
Is [what it is]
That [key benefit — why better than alternatives]
Unlike [main competitor]
We [your unique difference]

YOUR UNFAIR ADVANTAGE (pick at least one):
□ Location — closer to customer than competitors
□ Price — cheaper because of lower costs
□ Quality — better product/service
□ Speed — faster delivery or service
□ Relationships — trusted by the community
□ Specialisation — focused on one thing competitors don't
□ Technology — using tools competitors don't
□ Supply — access to inputs competitors can't get
□ Service — better customer experience
```

## Output Format
Deliver complete market intelligence report with specific data for the user's product and location. Use real competitor names and realistic prices where possible. Mark estimates with [VERIFY].
