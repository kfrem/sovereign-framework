# /legal-setup — Business Registration, Compliance & Contracts

## Purpose
Navigate the legal requirements for any enterprise at any level — from a sole trader to a cooperative to a government programme. Covers registration, permits, licences, tax obligations, contracts, and ongoing compliance. Designed for contexts where regulatory systems are complex, sometimes corrupt, and often poorly documented.

## Trigger
User says: /legal-setup [business type and country/location]

## Input Required
ONE sentence. Examples:
- "Register a poultry farm in Kumasi, Ghana"
- "Set up a cooperative in Nairobi"
- "Formalise my tailoring business in Lagos"
- "What permits do I need for a restaurant in Accra"

### STEP 4 — Essential Contracts

```
CONTRACTS YOU NEED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT (if hiring)
   Must include:
   □ Job title and duties
   □ Working hours
   □ Salary and payment date
   □ Leave entitlement
   □ Notice period
   □ Grounds for termination
   □ Confidentiality clause
   □ Signed by both parties

2. SUPPLIER AGREEMENT
   Must include:
   □ What is being supplied
   □ Quality standards
   □ Price and payment terms
   □ Delivery schedule
   □ Penalty for late delivery
   □ Dispute resolution method

3. CUSTOMER CONTRACT / TERMS OF SERVICE
   Must include:
   □ What you provide
   □ What the customer pays
   □ Payment terms
   □ Delivery/service timeline
   □ Warranty / guarantee
   □ Complaint process
   □ Refund policy

4. PARTNERSHIP / SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT
   (If applicable) Must include:
   □ Capital contribution of each partner
   □ Profit/loss sharing ratio
   □ Roles and responsibilities
   □ Decision-making process
   □ What happens if a partner wants to leave
   □ What happens if partners disagree
   □ Dispute resolution (arbitration, not courts)

5. LEASE AGREEMENT (if renting premises)
   Must include:
   □ Rent amount and payment date
   □ Duration and renewal terms
   □ Who pays for repairs
   □ Notice period for termination
   □ What alterations are allowed
   □ Security deposit terms

RULE: Every significant business relationship
needs a written agreement. Verbal agreements
destroy businesses and friendships.
```
