# /hiring — Recruitment, Team Building & Performance Management

## Purpose
Help any enterprise hire the right people, set clear expectations, manage performance, and build a team culture that prevents the #1 operational failure in African small businesses: people problems. Works for hiring your first employee or building a team of 50.

## Trigger
User says: /hiring [what role or team they need]

## Input Required
ONE sentence. Examples:
- "I need to hire my first shop assistant"
- "The cooperative needs a manager"
- "We're scaling and need 10 field agents"
- "I need a driver and two kitchen staff"

### STEP 2 — Recruitment Process

```
HOW TO FIND THE RIGHT PERSON
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SOURCING (Where to look):
□ Word of mouth (most reliable in Africa — ask trusted people)
□ Community notice boards (church, mosque, market)
□ WhatsApp groups (community, alumni, trade)
□ Local radio announcement
□ Job boards (Jobberman, BrighterMonday, etc.)
□ University/TVET career offices
□ Trade associations

SCREENING PROCESS:
Step 1: Collect applications (CV/letter or verbal for informal roles)
Step 2: Screen against must-have requirements (eliminate quickly)
Step 3: Interview top 3-5 candidates

INTERVIEW QUESTIONS:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Tell me about yourself and what you've done before│
│    (Reveals: communication, experience, honesty)     │
│                                                       │
│ 2. Why do you want this job specifically?             │
│    (Reveals: motivation, understanding of the role)  │
│                                                       │
│ 3. Describe a time you had to deal with a difficult  │
│    situation at work. What happened?                  │
│    (Reveals: problem-solving, temperament)           │
│                                                       │
│ 4. If a customer is angry and shouting at you, what  │
│    do you do?                                         │
│    (Reveals: customer service instinct)              │
│                                                       │
│ 5. If you notice a colleague stealing from the       │
│    business, what do you do?                          │
│    (Reveals: integrity, loyalty to business)         │
│                                                       │
│ 6. What questions do you have for me?                │
│    (Reveals: seriousness, preparation)               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

PRACTICAL TEST (Always — for every role):
□ Shop assistant: Handle a mock customer transaction
□ Driver: Drive the actual route
□ Cook: Prepare a test meal
□ Accounts: Process a mock day's transactions
□ Manager: Present a plan for the first week
□ Field agent: Conduct a mock field visit

REFERENCE CHECK (Always — minimum 2):
□ Call previous employer (if any)
□ Call community reference (pastor, teacher, elder)
□ Ask: "Would you hire this person again?"

DECISION CRITERIA:
  60% Character (honest, reliable, teachable)
  25% Skill (can they do the job?)
  15% Experience (have they done it before?)

  WHY CHARACTER FIRST: You can teach skills.
  You cannot teach integrity.
```

### STEP 3 — Onboarding (First 2 Weeks)

```
NEW HIRE ONBOARDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DAY 1:
□ Welcome and introduction to team
□ Tour of workplace
□ Explain the business: what we do, who we serve, why it matters
□ Review job description together — confirm understanding
□ Review rules and expectations (see Step 4)
□ Set up their workspace/tools
□ Assign a buddy/mentor for first week
□ Sign employment agreement

WEEK 1:
□ Shadow experienced colleague for 2-3 days
□ Supervised practice for 2-3 days
□ Daily 10-minute check-in with manager
□ End of week review: What's clear? What's confusing?

WEEK 2:
□ Work independently with close supervision
□ Daily check-ins continue
□ Manager observes and provides feedback
□ End of Week 2 assessment:
  □ Understands the role? □ Yes □ Needs more time
  □ Meets quality standards? □ Yes □ Needs training
  □ Fits the team? □ Yes □ Concern: ___
  □ On track for probation success? □ Yes □ At risk
```

### STEP 4 — Rules, Expectations & Culture

```
WORKPLACE RULES (Share on Day 1, Post on the Wall)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES:
1. Be on time. Every day.
2. Be honest. Always. About everything.
3. Treat every customer with respect.
4. Never take what isn't yours. Zero tolerance.
5. Keep the workplace clean and organised.
6. Report problems immediately — don't hide them.
7. Business money is not personal money.
8. Follow the quality standards — no shortcuts.
9. Respect every colleague regardless of position.
10. If you don't know, ask. Don't guess.

CONSEQUENCES:
  First warning: Verbal, documented
  Second warning: Written warning
  Third warning: Final written warning
  Fourth: Termination

  INSTANT DISMISSAL (No warnings):
  - Theft
  - Fraud
  - Violence
  - Gross dishonesty
  - Endangering others

  These rules apply equally to everyone,
  including family members.
```

### STEP 6 — Compensation Design

```
PAY STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRINCIPLES:
□ Pay fairly — at or above market rate for the role
□ Pay on time — never late, never partial
□ Pay transparently — everyone knows the pay structure
□ Pay for performance — reward good work visibly

STRUCTURE OPTIONS:
□ Fixed salary (stable, easy to budget)
□ Base + commission (motivates sales)
□ Base + performance bonus (motivates quality/output)
□ Daily wage (flexible, for casual work)
□ Piece rate (output-based, for production)

MARKET RATE RESEARCH:
  Ask 3-5 businesses in the area what they pay for similar roles
  Check online job listings for salary ranges
  Use /country-data for minimum wage and sector averages

RULE: If you can't afford to pay fairly, you're not
ready to hire. Underpaying guarantees high turnover,
theft, and poor service — which costs more than paying
a fair wage.
```
