# /stakeholder-map — Stakeholder Analysis & Engagement Design

## Purpose
Map every person, institution, and power structure that can help, block, or be affected by an enterprise — then design specific engagement strategies for each. This skill addresses the reality that in African contexts, business success depends as much on relationships, permissions, and community acceptance as on the product itself.

## Trigger
User says: /stakeholder-map [business or project description]

## What This Skill Solves
- **Political gatekeepers blocking progress** — chief, MP, district officer, regulatory body
- **Community resistance** — enterprise launches without social licence, faces opposition
- **Hidden power structures** — formal authority says yes, but informal power says no
- **Missed allies** — potential supporters never identified or engaged
- **Corruption exposure** — not knowing who will seek bribes, and how to navigate ethically
- **Ethnic / clan dynamics** — enterprise perceived as benefiting one group over another
- **Regulatory ambush** — permits, licences, inspections used as extraction tools
- **Competitor sabotage** — existing businesses undermining new entrants
- **Donor / NGO interference** — well-meaning organisations distorting local markets

## Doctrine Alignment
- **Asset-first**: Relationships and social capital are assets to be mapped before any plan is made
- **Anti-corruption**: Explicitly designs ethical engagement — no bribery, no patronage dependency
- **Three-population**: Identifies which stakeholders are workforce, market, or leadership

## Input Required
ONE sentence minimum. Examples:
- "I want to start a poultry farm in Kumasi"
- "The cooperative wants to export cocoa directly"
- "District assembly wants to build a market centre"
- "I'm a student with a tech idea in Lagos"

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Process              Who Extracts    Typical Ask      │
│ ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────│
│ Business registration                                │
│ Land allocation                                       │

│ Environmental clearance                               │
│ Tax registration                                      │

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

### STEP 5 — Social Licence Checklist
What the community needs to see before they accept your enterprise:

```
SOCIAL LICENCE REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ Local employment: How many local people will you hire?
□ Local procurement: What will you buy locally?
□ Environmental impact: What waste, noise, traffic do you create?
□ Cultural respect: Does your enterprise respect local customs?
□ Fair pricing: Are you charging fairly or extracting?
□ Transparency: Can the community see your operations?
□ Grievance mechanism: How can people complain?
□ Benefit sharing: How does the community gain beyond jobs?
□ Youth inclusion: Are young people included?
□ Gender inclusion: Are women included and respected?

RULE: If you cannot check at least 7 of 10 boxes,
redesign the enterprise before launching.
```

## Output Format
Deliver a complete stakeholder map with engagement calendar. Name specific roles/positions relevant to the user's context. Include the ethics navigation section for every enterprise without exception.
