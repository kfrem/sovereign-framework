# /digital-launch — Digital Presence & Marketing

## Purpose
Get any enterprise found by customers online and offline. From a market trader who needs WhatsApp Business to a cooperative that needs a Google listing, to a government programme that needs public awareness. Designed for the reality of African digital infrastructure — mobile-first, data-cost-conscious, and practical.

## Trigger
User says: /digital-launch [business description]

## Input Required
ONE sentence. Examples:
- "I sell fashion in Accra and need customers"
- "Our cooperative exports honey and needs buyers"
- "New restaurant opening next month"
- "Training programme needs to attract applicants"

### STEP 2 — Free Digital Foundation (Cost: Zero)
Things every business should set up immediately, at no cost:

```
FREE DIGITAL SETUP CHECKLIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIORITY 1: WhatsApp Business (Day 1)
□ Download WhatsApp Business (free)
□ Set up business profile:
  - Business name
  - Category
  - Description (what you sell, where)
  - Address
  - Business hours
  - Email
□ Set up catalogue (photos + prices of products)
□ Set up quick replies for common questions
□ Set up greeting message for new contacts
□ Create broadcast lists for:
  - Regular customers
  - Potential customers
  - Suppliers

PRIORITY 2: Google Business Profile (Day 1-2)
□ Go to business.google.com
□ Create listing:
  - Accurate business name
  - Correct address (appears on Google Maps)
  - Phone number
  - Business hours
  - Category
  - Photos (minimum 5: storefront, products, inside)
  - Description
□ This is FREE and makes you findable on Google Search and Maps

PRIORITY 3: Facebook Business Page (Day 2-3)
□ Create a Facebook Page (not personal profile)
□ Complete all information:
  - Profile photo (logo or best product)
  - Cover photo (your shop/products)
  - About section (what you do, where, contact)
  - Call-to-action button (WhatsApp, Call, or Message)
□ Post 3-5 photos of your best products
□ Invite friends and customers to like the page

PRIORITY 4: Instagram Business Account (Day 3)
□ Create or convert to Business account
□ Same photos and info as Facebook
□ Link to WhatsApp Business
□ Use local hashtags: #[City]Business #[Product]Ghana etc.
```

### STEP 3 — Content Strategy (What to Post)

```
CONTENT CALENDAR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

POST 3-5 TIMES PER WEEK. Here's what to post:

MONDAY: Product/Service Showcase
  Photo of your best product with price
  "Available now at [location]. Order via WhatsApp [number]"

TUESDAY: Behind the Scenes
  Show how you make/prepare your product
  People trust businesses they can see working

WEDNESDAY: Customer Story / Testimonial
  Happy customer photo (with permission)
  "Thank you [name] for choosing us!"

THURSDAY: Educational / Tips
  Share useful knowledge related to your product
  Positions you as the expert

FRIDAY: Promotion / Special Offer
  Weekend deal, bundle offer, or new arrival
  Create urgency: "This weekend only" or "While stock lasts"

CONTENT RULES:
□ Every post must have a CLEAR photo (natural light, clean background)
□ Every post must include HOW TO BUY (WhatsApp number, location)
□ Keep text short — 2-3 sentences maximum
□ Use local language where appropriate
□ Respond to EVERY comment and message (within 2 hours)
□ Never post blurry photos, political content, or complaints about customers

PHOTO TIPS (No professional camera needed):
□ Use natural daylight (near window or outside)
□ Clean, simple background (white cloth works)
□ Steady hand or lean phone against something
□ Show products in use, not just on a table
□ Include people when possible (trust signal)
```

### STEP 5 — Customer Retention

```
KEEPING CUSTOMERS (Cheaper than finding new ones)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ Save every customer's number in WhatsApp Business
□ Send WhatsApp broadcast for new products (weekly max)
□ Remember regular customers by name
□ Offer loyalty reward (buy 10, get 1 free)
□ Follow up after purchase: "Was everything OK?"
□ Handle complaints immediately and generously
□ Birthday/holiday messages to regular customers
□ Exclusive early access for loyal customers
□ Never let a complaint go unresolved for >24 hours

COMPLAINT RESOLUTION PROTOCOL:
1. Listen without arguing
2. Apologise (even if you think you're right)
3. Offer solution immediately
4. Follow up to confirm satisfaction
5. Record the complaint (pattern detection)

A well-handled complaint creates more loyalty
than a trouble-free transaction.
```
