What Is The Sovereign Framework?

The Sovereign Framework is a system of 18 interconnected skills (tools) that solve one problem at every scale: turning what is idle, wasted, or untapped into profitable, sustainable economic activity that creates jobs, builds wealth, expands the tax base, and makes communities and governments self-sustaining.

Every skill starts from the same principle: audit what already exists before asking for anything new.

  • A community has land, skills, savings groups, natural resources, and social structures
  • A government ministry has buildings, staff, budgets, data, and legal mandates
  • A struggling business has equipment, customers, relationships, and a brand

The Framework makes what is invisible, visible — and then activates it.

2

The Five Sovereign Doctrines

These are the non-negotiable principles that govern every skill in the Framework:

1

Sovereign Assets First

Before designing anything, audit what already exists. The enterprise or programme is built on what the community, government, or business already has — not on what it needs to be given.

2

Market Revenue Mandate

Every solution must generate private market income. No single revenue source (including government) should exceed 60% of total revenue by Year 3. This is how you survive budget cuts.

3

People as Delivery

The unemployed are not the problem — they are the workforce. Three populations are always available: unemployed graduates, long-term unemployed adults, and retired professionals.

4

Profitable & Provable

Every programme must be self-sustaining within 24 months, or its social value must be proven with financial rigour. No vague "impact" claims — numbers or nothing.

5

Evidence-Based Everything

Every figure in every document must be verified against official sources. Exchange rates checked same-day. Government budget from current year. No stale data.

3

How To Use The Skills

With Claude Code (Anthropic's AI CLI)

Place the skill files from the skills/ folder into ~/.claude/commands/ and invoke them with slash commands:

/solve [describe your problem] /opportunity-scan [geography and sector] /community-enterprise [community description and enterprise idea] /financial-model [programme or business name]

With Other AI Platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.)

  • Open the relevant .md file from skills/
  • Copy the full contents
  • Paste it as the first message or system prompt
  • Replace $ARGUMENTS with your specific input
  • The AI will follow the skill's structure and produce the output

Important: Skills can be chained. The output of one skill feeds into the next. Always run /data-verify first when producing any document with financial figures for government or investors.

/solve The Master Consultant Orchestrator Master
▼
Fileskills/solve.md
CategoryMaster Orchestrator
ForAnyone with a complex problem

What it does: This is the brain of the Framework. You give it any complex, messy, multi-layered problem — and it diagnoses the problem, selects the right combination of other skills, puts them in the correct sequence, and produces an integrated solution.

What you give it: A problem description. Can be vague ("youth unemployment in the Northern Region") or specific ("Ministry of Employment has 47 dormant TVET centres, 12,000 registered unemployed graduates, and a GHS 50 million budget they can't spend").

What it gives you back:

  • Problem Diagnosis — What is actually happening, across 5 dimensions (nature, geography, urgency, complexity, audience)
  • Three Critical Questions answered — What assets exist? What is the path to self-sufficiency? Where does the workforce come from?
  • Skill Selection — Which skills to use and in what order
  • Full Integrated Solution using 3-layer architecture: Foundation (assets), Engine (people), Sustainability (revenue)
  • Critical Path — Numbered steps with owners and timelines
  • Risk Register — What could go wrong and how to prevent it
  • One-Page Master Brief — Visual summary

Key rules: Never recommends external funding as a starting point. Always starts with what already exists. Market revenue embedded from Day 1. Every solution must have a self-sustaining financial model.

When to use: When the problem is too big or complex for a single skill. When you don't know which skill to start with. When the solution needs to combine government, community, workforce, and financial elements.

/asset-audit Sovereign Asset Strategist Discovery
▼
Fileskills/asset-audit.md
CategoryDiscovery
ForGovernment, organisations, communities

What it does: Audits EVERYTHING a government, organisation, or community already owns — and calculates how much idle value is sitting there unused. This is always the first step before designing any programme or enterprise.

What you give it: Country, ministry or organisation, geographic region, and what you're trying to do.

What it gives you back:

  • Physical Asset Inventory — Land, buildings, equipment, vehicles (with location, current use, % utilisation, repurposing options)
  • Human Capital Asset Inventory — Existing staff for redeployment, retired professionals, community structures (SUSU groups, women's associations)
  • Programmatic Asset Inventory — Active and dormant government programmes, legal mandates, existing data/research
  • Relational Asset Inventory — Government contacts, NGO relationships, community trust networks
  • Financial Asset Inventory — Unspent budgets, revenue-generating assets not being monetised
  • Asset Utilisation Matrix — Every asset rated 0–100% used, with repurposing priority
  • Repurposing Blueprint — Specific plan for each high-priority underutilised asset
  • Total Value Unlocked — Combined value in local currency
  • 90-Day Quick Wins — Zero-cost actions that prove value immediately
  • One-Page Brief — Visual summary

The key insight: The phrase "we don't have the budget" almost always means "we haven't audited what we already have." This skill makes the invisible visible.

When to use: ALWAYS first. Before any programme design, business plan, government proposal, or investment case.

/opportunity-scan Economic Intelligence Analyst Discovery
▼
Fileskills/opportunity-scan.md
CategoryDiscovery
ForPlanners, entrepreneurs, communities

What it does: Scans any geography — a district, region, or country — and identifies every viable business opportunity that can be started using what already exists in that place.

What you give it: A geography (country, region, district), optionally a sector focus, who will act on the opportunities, and what assets are already known.

Eight Categories of Opportunities:

  • Import Substitution — What does this area buy from outside that could be made locally?
  • Value Addition — What raw products leave unprocessed that could be processed here?
  • Underserved Markets — Who has money but can't find a local supplier?
  • Government Spending — What does government spend that local businesses could supply?
  • Natural Resources — Forests, rivers, clay, medicinal plants — how to commercialise?
  • Skills-Based Services — What can local people do that others will pay for?
  • Digital/Platform Economy — Phone-based work with minimal startup
  • Circular Economy — Waste into resource

Also includes: Opportunity Ranking (top 5 scored), Implementation Gateway (90-day action plan), and One-Page Brief.

When to use: Before choosing an enterprise for a community. Before designing a government economic programme. When an entrepreneur asks "what business should I start here?"

ForStruggling businesses, lenders

What it does: Diagnoses why a struggling business is failing and prescribes a structured 90-day rescue plan. Core insight: most "failures" are actually asset misallocation problems.

7 Failure Modes It Diagnoses:

  • Pricing too low (busy but broke)
  • Working capital trap (profitable on paper, no cash)

  • Founder dependency (everything depends on one person)
  • Cost/revenue mismatch (costs grow faster than income)
  • Unstructured growth (expanded without systems)

What it gives you back:

  • Health Diagnostic — 10 dimensions rated 1–10 with rescue verdict
  • Root Cause Analysis — Which failure mode
  • Business Asset Audit — Underutilised assets
  • Revenue Redesign — Restructured streams
  • Cost Restructure — Cut without killing capacity
  • People Redesign — Team assessment + 3-population injection
  • Government Programme Connection — Fastest revenue stabiliser
  • Debt Restructure — Creditor proposals
  • 90-Day Rescue Plan — Week-by-week actions

The 18 skills form an integrated system. Here are the common workflows:

The Master Sequence

/data-verify → Verify all data first
/asset-audit → Map what already exists
/gov-proposal or /workforce-engine → Depending on need
/financial-model → Full economics
/social-value (if needed for non-commercial components)
